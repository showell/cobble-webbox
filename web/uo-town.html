Try it

  1. Start the day. The server ticks the town four times a second (tw-tick); Nell, Jorin and Mira keep their schedules and the shops open at eight.
  2. Join the town. The server makes you a body at the inn (tw-join).
  3. Walk into a shop. Press an open shop (or tap the map); the server walks your body there by the townsfolk's own router (rte-toward), and its keeper greets you. An open shop is lit, rimmed in gold; a shut one is dark.
  4. Buy something. In the shop, say “vendor buy” for the keeper's prices, then press Buy (ns-handler-at, gv-handle-at). Join first.
  5. Send a resident on an errand. Tap Nell, Jorin or Mira on the map, then a Send button: they leave their schedule for a game hour (tl-direct).
